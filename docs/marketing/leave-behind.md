# What I do, and what it costs

I help small businesses stop doing the same job by hand over and over.
One person, not an agency, so you deal with me start to finish.

## What it costs

Flat prices, agreed before anything starts. No hourly billing and no
surprise line items.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Connecting two tools** so you type things once | **from $500** |
| **Automating one repetitive job**: reminders, follow-ups, invoices | **from $750** |
| **An assistant that answers** after hours | **from $1,000** |
| **Straight answers from your own numbers** | **from $1,200** |
| **A new website** built to bring customers in | **from $2,000** |

## How it goes

1. **A free call.** Half an hour. If I cannot help, I say so on the call.
2. **A written quote.** One page, one flat number, good for 30 days.
3. **I build it**, with updates as pieces land, and you see it working
   before the final invoice.

## What I will not do

Sell you something you do not need. A job you do twice a year, or one
that changes every time, costs more to automate than to keep doing by
hand. Nor will I touch the part your customers actually come for.

**The free call is the whole first step.** Book a time, email, or ring me.
