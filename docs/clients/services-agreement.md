# Services Agreement

Between **Sebastian Inman** ("I", "me"), based in Southern Oregon, and
**{{CLIENT_NAME}}** of **{{CLIENT_BUSINESS}}** ("you"), dated
**{{DATE}}**.

This agreement covers the project described in the attached proposal
dated {{PROPOSAL_DATE}}. The proposal says what I'm building and what
it costs; this page says how we'll work together.

**1. The work.** I'll build what the proposal describes. If either of
us wants to change the scope along the way, we'll agree on the change
and any price difference in writing (email counts) before the work or
the bill changes.

**2. Price and payment.** The price is the flat amount in the
proposal: {{FLAT_PRICE}}. {{DEPOSIT_PORTION}} is due before work
starts, and the rest is due within {{PAYMENT_DAYS}} days of delivery.
Invoices come by email. The price doesn't include third-party
subscriptions you sign up for (your website hosting, software plans),
which are yours and stay in your name and control.

**3. What I need from you.** The proposal lists what I need (logins,
content, decisions). If those arrive late, the timeline moves with
them; the price doesn't change.

**4. Who owns what.** When the final payment clears, you own the work
I built for you: the website, the automations, the configurations, the
content. Accounts are set up in your name from day one wherever
possible. I keep ownership of my own pre-existing tools and general
know-how, and you get a permanent license to anything of mine that's
part of your project. I may describe the project (without your private
data) as an example of my work; tell me if you'd rather I didn't.

**5. Your data.** Anything I learn about your business stays between
us. I only access what the project needs, and I return or delete
client credentials when the project wraps.

**6. If something breaks.** For {{SUPPORT_WINDOW}} after delivery,
I'll fix anything that doesn't work the way the proposal said, free.
After that, fixes and changes are new work we'd scope together. I
build carefully, but I can't promise third-party services (the tools
your project connects) will never change or break; when they do, I'll
tell you what happened and what fixing it takes.

**7. If it isn't working out.** Either of us can end the project with
written notice. You pay for work completed up to that point; anything
already paid for gets delivered in whatever state it's in, documented
honestly.

**8. The boring but important part.** I'm an independent contractor,
not an employee. My total liability under this agreement is capped at
the amount you paid me for the project. This agreement is governed by
Oregon law, and anything we can't resolve by talking gets resolved in
{{COUNTY}} County, Oregon.
