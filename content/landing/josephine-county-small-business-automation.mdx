## Big county, small crews

Josephine County covers a lot of ground: Grants Pass and its river economy, the farms and homesteads down the Illinois Valley, Cave Junction and the road to the Oregon Caves. What ties it together is small businesses run by small crews, often miles from the nearest "IT guy," doing their own paperwork after the real work is done.

That distance is exactly why automation fits here. Systems that run themselves don't care how far out you are: invoices follow up on their own, bookings confirm themselves, and after-hours questions get answered whether you're in town or up a forest road.
