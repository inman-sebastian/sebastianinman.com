## Your business is full of answers. Let's go get them.

Your sales records, invoices, and customer lists already know which products make you money, which customers keep coming back, and which slow season is coming. But that knowledge is buried in files and programs you don't have time to dig through.

I use AI to turn your existing records into plain-English answers and simple reports you'll actually read.

**Sound familiar?**

- You make decisions on gut feel because pulling the numbers takes too long
- You couldn't quickly say which service or product is your most profitable
- End-of-month reporting means a painful night with spreadsheets
- Big companies have analysts for this; you have a shoebox of receipts and a busy week

**What you get**

- A simple dashboard or regular report showing the numbers that matter to *your* business
- Plain-English summaries — "Tuesdays are your quietest day; here's what that costs you" — not walls of charts
- Answers on demand: ask a question about your business, get a straight answer from your own data

**A good fit if…** you have at least a year of sales or customer records and a nagging feeling you're leaving money on the table.
