## Answer every customer, even the ones who call at 9pm

Half of small business inquiries come in after hours or while you're with another customer. Most of them ask the same handful of questions: Are you open? Do you do X? How much does it cost? How do I book?

An AI assistant is like a well-trained front-desk person who never sleeps. It knows your business, answers in your voice, and hands the conversation to you the moment it needs a human.
