## Answer every customer, even the ones who call at 9pm

Half of small business inquiries come in after hours or while you're with another customer. Most of them ask the same handful of questions: Are you open? Do you do X? How much does it cost? How do I book?

An AI assistant is like a well-trained front-desk person who never sleeps. It knows your business, answers in your voice, and hands the conversation to you the moment it needs a human.

**Sound familiar?**

- You miss inquiries because you can't answer the phone while doing the actual work
- You type the same answers to the same questions every single week
- Leads go cold overnight because nobody replied until morning
- You've seen chatbots on big-company sites and figured that was out of reach

**What you get**

- An assistant trained on *your* business: your services, prices, hours, and policies
- Set up on your website, and where it makes sense, your text messages or social pages
- Clear guardrails: it only says what you've approved, and it knows when to hand off to you
- A simple way to review conversations and improve answers over time

**A good fit if…** you regularly miss inquiries, or repetitive customer questions eat into your working day.
