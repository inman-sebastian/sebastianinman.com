## Hi, I'm Sebastian.

I'm a software developer based in Southern Oregon, and I help small businesses get technology help that actually fits them: their budget, their tools, and the way they work.

I've spent 13 years as a senior web developer building websites, integrations, and automations for companies of every size, in everything from finance to manufacturing to e-commerce. Most of that work has been freelance, which means most of my career has looked like this: sit down with someone, figure out what they actually need, build exactly that.

Along the way I noticed something: the businesses that need this help the most are the least likely to get it. There's no IT department at a bakery or a two-truck contracting outfit. There's an owner doing payroll at 10pm.

That's the gap I work in.

## What working with me looks like

No jargon, no upselling, no six-month projects. We start with a free conversation about your business. What's eating your time, what's falling through the cracks, what you wish "just worked." Then I tell you honestly what's worth fixing, what it costs, and what you'd get back.

Every project gets scoped with the same goal: to pay for itself, by giving you your hours back and stopping leads from slipping away. If I don't think the numbers will work out for you, I'll say so before we start.

## Why Southern Oregon

I've lived in Southern Oregon for 13 years, the same 13 I've spent building software, and that's no coincidence: my first professional development job was right here, and the whole career since was built in the Rogue Valley. I'd rather help the businesses in my own community first: the shops, contractors, farms, and services that make this place what it is. I work with clients anywhere in the United States, but if you're local, coffee's on me for our first meeting.
