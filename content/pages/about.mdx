## Hi, I'm Sebastian.

I'm a software developer based in Southern Oregon, and I help small businesses get technology help that actually fits them: their budget, their tools, and the way they work.

{/* VERIFY: years of experience (placeholder claim below) */}
I've spent over a decade building software: websites, integrations, and automations for companies large and small. Along the way I noticed something: the businesses that needed this help the most were the least likely to get it. There's no IT department at a bakery or a two-truck contracting outfit. There's an owner doing payroll at 10pm.

That's the gap I work in.

## What working with me looks like

No jargon, no upselling, no six-month projects. We start with a free conversation about your business. What's eating your time, what's falling through the cracks, what you wish "just worked." Then I tell you honestly what's worth fixing, what it costs, and what you'd get back.

Most of my projects pay for themselves within a few months, because the goal is always the same: give you your hours back and stop leads from slipping away.
